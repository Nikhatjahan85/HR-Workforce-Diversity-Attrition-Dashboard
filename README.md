## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Workforce Diversity & Attrition Dashboard** is an interactive HR Analytics solution developed in **Tableau** to analyze workforce diversity, employee attrition, employee satisfaction, compensation trends, and workforce risk indicators.

The dashboard consolidates critical HR metrics into a single executive-level reporting solution, enabling HR professionals and business leaders to monitor workforce health, identify retention challenges, evaluate diversity initiatives, and make informed workforce planning decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations often face challenges in retaining talent, maintaining workforce diversity, and understanding employee engagement. HR data is frequently scattered across multiple systems, making it difficult to identify workforce trends and attrition risks.

This dashboard addresses these challenges by:

• Centralizing key HR metrics

• Monitoring employee attrition trends

• Evaluating workforce diversity

• Identifying high-risk employees

• Tracking employee satisfaction levels

• Supporting workforce planning and retention strategies

## 🎯 Project Objectives

• Analyze workforce diversity across departments

• Monitor employee attrition and turnover trends

• Identify employees at high attrition risk
